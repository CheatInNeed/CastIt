Castit manual test page

Public test streams, one scenario per section. Play one, then open the Castit popup. Reload the page between scenarios for a clean candidate list.

archive.org/download/BigBuckBunny_124/…/big_buck_bunny_720p_surround.mp4

Expect: one FILE · MP4 · 360p candidate (the archive.org URL, not the dn….archive.org redirect target). It can take a few seconds to load.

2. HLS through hls.js (MediaSource / blob:)

test-streams.mux.dev/x36xhzz/x36xhzz.m3u8

Expect: one HLS candidate with "N qualities", 1080p, H.264. Variant playlists are hidden after the popup inspects the master.

3. DASH through dash.js (MediaSource / blob:)

dash.akamaized.net/akamai/bbb_30fps/bbb_30fps.mpd

Expect: one DASH candidate, 1080p, linked to the playing blob: player.

4. Cross-origin embedded player (iframe on 127.0.0.1, delayed init)

The iframe is a different origin than this page. Its player only creates the stream when you press its play button.

Expect: HLS candidate shown as "… · in player 127.0.0.1" and associated with the iframe's MediaSource player.

5. Dynamically inserted player

Expect: the Sintel MP4 appears in the popup without reopening it.

6. Player inside a closed shadow root

Expect: the Sintel trailer is detected even though the page cannot see into the closed shadow root.

7. Short "ad-like" clip in a small player

Expect: ranked below the real content (short clip, small player, tiny file).

8. Broken source (HTTP 403)

Expect: listed as "Unlikely": the page's own player could not load it (HTTP 403). Never pre-selected.
